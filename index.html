About This Research

Research Methodology

This family history has been compiled through extensive research of public records, family documents, newspaper archives, and oral histories. Sources include Leon County records, Florida State Archives, VMI alumni records, and family correspondence spanning seven generations.

Family Historians

This website is maintained by the Lewis family descendants and genealogy researchers. For corrections, additional information, or research inquiries, please contact the family historians.
